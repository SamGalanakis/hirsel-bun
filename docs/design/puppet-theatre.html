WHY THIS IS INTERESTING

Visible Control

The puppeteer above with strings to each worker makes the orchestration relationship literal and visible. You see the shepherd controlling workers from above. Taut strings mean active work, slack strings mean idle. The power structure is spatial.

Acts Give Narrative

Specs as acts in a play create a natural sequence and sense of dramatic progression. Act I, II, III — each builds on the last. Validated acts receive applause. The whole run becomes a performance with a beginning, middle, and end.

Animation Carries State

Dancing marionettes are immediately legible as active workers. Limp, hanging marionettes read as idle. The spotlight illuminates the current focus. No labels needed — the animation vocabulary maps directly to worker state through physical metaphor.

The Curtain Is Dispatch

Dispatch raises the curtain on a new act. The audience watches the performance unfold. When all acts are done, the curtain call begins — delivery. Theatre has centuries of visual language for "prepare, perform, conclude" that maps perfectly to plan, execute, ship.
