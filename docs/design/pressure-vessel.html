WHY THIS IS INTERESTING

Pressure = Workload

A run under too much pressure (too many active tasks, too few workers) literally looks stressed — particles vibrate, caution lights blink, gauges climb. You feel the system state.

Gauges are real metrics

System pressure = task queue depth. Throughput = tasks completed per hour. Token burn = LLM cost. Relief valves = repair cycles. Every industrial metaphor maps to a real measurement.

Control room aesthetic

Nuclear plant control rooms are designed for situation awareness. Riveted panels, warning lights, gauge clusters. Hirsel runs ARE industrial processes — treating them like one makes the stakes visible.

Particles show parallelism

Unlike list views, the particle viewport shows all tasks simultaneously in a spatial cluster. High-pressure ones bounce near workers. Stable ones settle. You see the whole system's energy state.
