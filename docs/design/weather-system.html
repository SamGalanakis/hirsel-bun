WHY THIS IS INTERESTING

Pressure = Progress

Low-pressure storm systems are where the work happens — turbulent, energetic, drawing resources in. High-pressure zones are calm validated completions. The pressure metaphor maps perfectly to spec lifecycle: instability resolves into stability.

Fronts show boundaries

Cold fronts advancing across the chart are dependency chains — they push forward and force change. Occluded fronts are blocked specs, trapped between competing pressures. The meteorological vocabulary makes state transitions spatial and visceral.

Jet streams are workers

Workers flow between systems like jet streams — fast, high-altitude rivers of energy that connect weather systems. You can see which workers service which specs, and how the work flows across the board as animated dashed lines.

Delivery is the forecast

A weather system clears. The forecast bar shows the approaching calm — delivery. "Clearing skies" means validated specs, merged PRs, stable production. The whole metaphor has a natural conclusion that maps to shipping code.
