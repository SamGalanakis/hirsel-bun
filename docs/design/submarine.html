WHY THIS IS INTERESTING

Depth = complexity

Submarines dive deeper for harder missions. Specs naturally sort by complexity — validated work surfaces for delivery while harder problems are tackled at depth. The vertical axis creates an intuitive progress metaphor: rising toward the surface means approaching completion.

Sonar = awareness

A sonar scope reveals the invisible. Tasks are hidden underwater work — blips on the screen confirm they exist and show their position relative to the sub. The periodic ping sweep mirrors hirsel's polling for worker status updates.

Torpedo tubes = workers

Workers are weapons systems: loaded, aimed, and fired at specific targets. Each tube handles one task at a time. The metaphor captures the precision and parallelism of worker dispatch — multiple tubes firing simultaneously at different objectives.

Ballast = priority control

Blowing ballast tanks forces a submarine to surface. In hirsel, surfacing validated specs means pushing them to delivery. The ballast metaphor makes priority tangible — you control buoyancy to decide what rises to the top and what stays submerged.
