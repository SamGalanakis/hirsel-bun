Why this is interesting

Depth = maturity

Foundational specs settle to the bottom. Newest work is loose topsoil at the surface. This inverts the typical chronological list — you read from bedrock up, which matches how foundations support everything above.

Fossils = completed tasks

Tasks don't disappear when done — they become fossils embedded in the stratum. You can see what work was required to form each layer. The record is geological, not just a checkmark.

Workers as forces of nature

A worker isn't a card or a dot — it's a magma intrusion, a volcanic force pushing upward through the strata. When a worker hits a blocked layer, you see the pressure building below it. The metaphor creates genuine drama.

Crystallization = validation

Validated specs don't just get a green checkmark. They crystallize — growing crystal formations that are visually distinct from unvalidated strata. The longer something has been validated, the more crystals grow.
