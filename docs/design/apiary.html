WHY THIS IS INTERESTING

Hexagons Are Efficient

Honeycomb is nature's most space-efficient tiling. Specs as hexagonal cells create a dense, beautiful grid where every cell touches its neighbors. The geometry implies collaboration — adjacent cells share walls, just like specs share dependencies.

Honey Fill Shows Progress

Tasks filling a cell with amber honey from the bottom up is an immediately legible progress indicator. Half-full means half-done. Full means ready for capping. The viscous, warm fill animation makes progress feel organic and satisfying.

Waggle Dance IS Dispatch

Real bees return to the hive and perform a waggle dance to tell others where to find resources. This maps perfectly to dispatch — the system analyzing the board and directing workers to unclaimed tasks. The biological metaphor is not decoration; it is the architecture.

Colony Intelligence

No single bee knows the plan. The hive emerges from simple rules: find work, do work, report back. Hirsel workers operate the same way — claim a task, execute it, complete it. The queen (shepherd) coaches but does not micromanage. The colony is the orchestrator.
