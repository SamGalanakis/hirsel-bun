Why this is interesting

Specs literally are programs

A Jacquard punch card was the first stored program. Specs in Hirsel encode intent that machines execute — the parallel is exact. Holes in the card determine what gets woven. The spec's structure determines what tasks get created.

Sequential card reading

The loom reads one card at a time through the reader head. This maps perfectly to how dispatch processes specs in dependency order. You can see which card is currently being read and which are queued. Blocked cards wait their turn.

Fabric is the deliverable

The woven output IS the code — the delivery artifact. Different colored threads represent different types of work: indigo for data, crimson for logic, gold for configuration. The pattern emerges row by row as tasks complete.

Shuttles are workers

Each shuttle carries a specific thread back and forth through the warp. Workers in Hirsel carry out specific task types. An active shuttle animates across the fabric. Idle shuttles sit in their bays waiting for the next card to feed.
