Direction 01

Mitosis

A spec is a living cell. When dispatched, the nucleus duplicates and the cell membrane stretches and splits — the spec undergoes mitosis into child tasks. Workers are enzymes operating inside each daughter cell. Delivery is exocytosis: the completed cell leaving the organism.

Cell = Spec or Task

The membrane shows status. The nucleus carries intent. Organelles are metadata floating in context.

Enzyme = Worker

Moves through the cell, transforming plan DNA into working code. Active enzymes drift and pulse.

Spindle = Dispatch

The division apparatus. When dispatch fires, spindle fibers pull the spec apart into tasks.

Why this is interesting

It maps perfectly

Hirsel's spec→task decomposition IS cell division. A single intent duplicates its DNA (plan), splits into independent execution units (tasks), and each gets its own worker (enzyme). The biology maps 1:1.

State is spatial

You don't read status from labels — you see it. Validated cells glow green at the membrane. Working cells have spinning status rings and visible enzymes. Pending cells are translucent. The whole organism breathes.

Scale reveals itself

A complex route would look like a tissue — dozens of cells at various stages of division, enzymes moving through them, some cells dying (failed specs) and being reabsorbed. The metaphor scales naturally.

Delivery is departure

Exocytosis: the cell packages its contents and ejects them from the organism. That's exactly what delivery does — packages the completed work and pushes it out of the hirsel into the repo.
