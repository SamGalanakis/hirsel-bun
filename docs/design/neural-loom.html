Why This Direction

Activation Semantics

The neural metaphor maps naturally to orchestration. Specs "activate" downstream tasks just as input neurons fire through connections. The propagation from left to right mirrors real data flow through the system.

Weight = Load

Connection weights communicate worker utilization at a glance. Thick, bright axons mean heavy load; dim connections are idle pathways. Users intuit system health from the visual density of the loom.

Pulse as Progress

Animated data packets flowing along connections create a living sense of progress. Each pulse is a unit of work moving through the pipeline. When pulses stop, something is blocked.

Layer Legibility

The strict layer structure prevents visual chaos. Even with many specs and tasks, the columnar layout keeps the information hierarchy clear: input, processing, routing, output.
