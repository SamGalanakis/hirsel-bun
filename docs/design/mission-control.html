Why This Direction

Operational Gravity

Mission control communicates seriousness and reliability. The GO/NO-GO pattern gives users a clear, binary read on system health. Every component either works or it doesn't -- no ambiguity.

Phase Awareness

The mission timeline maps directly to hirsel's lifecycle: init, decompose, execute, deliver. Users always know where they are in the process and what comes next.

Telemetry Density

The card grid packs maximum information into minimum space. Every metric has a value, trend bar, and status indicator. The phosphor green on dark navy is designed for sustained monitoring without eye strain.

Comm Loop as History

The right panel serves as a timestamped activity log that reads like flight controller chatter. Color-coded borders let users scan for alerts without reading every line. It's the narrative thread through the mission.
