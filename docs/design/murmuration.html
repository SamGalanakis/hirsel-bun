Why this is interesting

Emergent order from simple rules

A murmuration has no leader. Each bird follows three rules: align with neighbors, stay close, don't collide. Workers follow similar rules: claim available tasks, respect dependencies, don't duplicate effort. The beautiful pattern emerges from the protocol, not from a plan.

Cohesion = validation

A tight flock shimmering in unison is a validated spec. Every bird knows where it belongs. A loose, scattered formation is work in progress — the forces haven't resolved yet. You can see validation as a physical quality: how closely the parts hold together.

Movement IS the state

You don't need status labels when the dots themselves are moving. Drifting dots are working tasks. Still, tight clusters are done. A lone wandering dot is a draft. The animation carries all the information that a traditional status badge would — but you feel it instead of reading it.

Scale reveals beauty

One bird is nothing. A hundred birds doing the same thing is a murmuration. This metaphor rewards scale: a route with many specs and many tasks becomes a genuinely beautiful visualization. The more complex the work, the more striking the display.
