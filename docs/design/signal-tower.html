Why This Direction

Directional Attention

Dishes physically pointing at targets is the most intuitive representation of worker-to-spec assignment. The tilt angle communicates focus. A scanning dish is visibly searching for work, making idle workers legible.

Signal Strength Metaphor

Task completion as signal strength is immediately understandable. Strong signal means the worker is making progress. Weak signal means early stages. No signal is undecomposed noise. The spectrum bar at top gives the system-wide allocation view.

Noise Floor Insight

The flickering noise floor visualization makes undecomposed work feel like static interference -- something that needs to be resolved. It creates urgency around the decomposition step that flat task lists cannot.

Frequency Allocation

The spectrum bar across the top shows worker bandwidth allocation at a glance. It answers "who is working on what and how much capacity remains" in a single horizontal strip. The noise band on the right is unallocated potential.
