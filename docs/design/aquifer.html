Why this is interesting

Depth encodes dependency

Foundational specs drill deeper because they touch more of the system. A schema migration goes all the way down to bedrock. E2E tests sit near the surface. The physical depth of the well communicates how fundamental the spec is.

Water level shows progress

As tasks complete, water rises in the shaft. Half-full is half-done. Overflowing is validated. Empty is unstarted. You see completion as a physical substance accumulating, which is more visceral than a percentage bar.

Pumps are workers

A pump actively bobbing means a worker is pulling results out of the ground. Idle pumps are available workers. No pump at all means the well hasn't been equipped yet. The mechanical action makes invisible compute visible.

Strata are shared context

The water table and rock layers run behind all wells, showing that specs share underlying infrastructure. When one well taps the aquifer, it affects pressure in neighboring wells. Blocked specs are wells that can't reach water yet.
