Design Storm

26

How should Hirsel look and feel? These 26 directions are exploratory visual studies for a system with one shepherd, visible parallel threads, a shared canvas artifact, and dense execution feedback.

Many of the individual studies predate the current product naming and still use older spec or worker metaphors. Treat this gallery as design exploration, not as a literal architecture document.
