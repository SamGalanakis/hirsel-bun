Typography

ET Book - Tufte's Bembo
Highland Craft
ET Book brings scholarly gravitas to Hirsel - a Bembo-style serif designed by Edward Tufte for his books on data visualization. Optimized for clarity in dense information, it features beautiful oldstyle figures (1234567890) that blend naturally with text. The font stands apart from typical app interfaces while remaining supremely readable.
